Protocol design elements and USDM

Data or words?

A protocol is written for people. USDM aims to hold the same study design so that systems can use it. How far it gets depends on how much of each design element is held as data. This page asks, for five familiar design elements: which parts does USDM hold as data, and which are still words?

Two kinds of content

Example: an objective in USDM

Level: primaryIts endpoints, linked

The objective itself, as a sentence written in the protocol

Data Choices from a list, numbers, links. A system can check and reuse them.

Words Sentences and written rules. A person has to read them.

USDM also gives every design element its own identity. That alone does not make its content data.

Two links that are not in USDM

Endpoint to assessments

USDM does not say which activities or measurements in the schedule produce the data for an endpoint. The finding and its test

Design element to protocol section

Objectives, endpoints and eligibility criteria are not linked to the section of the protocol where they are written. The finding and its test

Both may be deliberate choices of scope. They are open questions, not errors.

In short

Held as data
The schedule of activities, and the arm and epoch grid
Held as words
Objectives, endpoints, eligibility criteria, and the rules inside elements
Not held
Endpoint to assessments, and design element to protocol section
