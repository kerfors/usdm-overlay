What USDM attributes and classes actually hold

usdm-overlay reads the USDM v4 model as rendered by usdm-rdf and classifies every attribute and class by the kind of content it carries. Every reading names its evidence. This page lets you browse those readings and the findings they surface.

Layer 2 · role per class

Browse the classes

A role describes what the class itself holds, counting its own and inherited attributes, without housekeeping, naming and notes. Pick a role, or search, then open a class.

Layer 3 · findings, each with a test

findings about the model

Each finding is rerun on every build. Its standing is separate from its evidence: a gap the model states can still be a deliberate design choice.
